# Two-minute click path

1. Open the app. Leave the access code blank.
2. Click **Load demo data**. The Danish Coffee Lab website text, brief, and eight ads fill in.
3. Click **Analyze coverage**. The run replays from cache. The cost line stays at $0 for those hits.
4. Read the summary bar. It should say 8 ads and 4 of 10 motivations, including Emotion/ritual.
5. Glance at the grid. The coloured cells are the top gaps. The Safety/trust × Testimonial cell says the testimonial is illustrative and must be replaced with a real customer quote.
6. Click one ad thumbnail. The panel shows why it was classified, then a Layout QA line for the lockup.
7. Scroll to **Before and after**. The eight ads are the before. The three concepts are the after, each with a Feed 4:5 and a Reels 9:16. Concept 2's vertical frame is labelled **AI-generated persona, fictional**. Concept 1 keeps **Illustrative testimonial: replace with a real customer quote before use.**
8. Click **Export Markdown** if you want the same comparison as a file.
9. Stop on the before/after view. That is the closing shot.
